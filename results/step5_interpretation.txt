Reading (hedged): the calorimetric jump is ~59 % of R ln 4 and 13-36 % below the strain-implied
mean-field value. That is consistent with entropy already removed above T_N by paramagnetic short-range
order in a strongly frustrated lattice (|θ_p|/T_N ≈ 6-9; θ_p = -1650 to -2600 K, Chaussy), and with the
broad tails. The single-order-parameter mean field cannot capture this; it is stated as a limitation.
